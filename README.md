# Retail-Sales-Analysis-
An excel project analyzing retail sales performance , profitability, and customer purchasing pattern
This project analyzes retail sales data to identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and understand business profitability.

OBJECTIVES
Evaluate total sales and operating profit.
Identify the best-performing products and locations.
Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
Compare sales performance across product categories.
Present key findings through an interactive-style dashboard
TOOLS
EXCEL
PIVOT TABLES
SPREADSHEETS
A CLEAR DASBOARDS
BASIC COMPUTERS FORMULAS
PROJECT PROCESS
Cleaned and organized the dataset.
Checked for duplicate records, incorrect values , missing rows, patterns 
Used formulas to calculate relevant business metrics.
Created pivot tables to summarize sales performance.
Developed charts and a dashboard to present the findings.
KEY FINDINGS 
The retail sales dashboard and charts clearly shows the total sales which is $120,166,650,and also the operating profit to be, $47,224,968,these profits and sales were made from the total sales of 2,478,861 units been sold. 
West gear has the highest performance among the retailers with high sales, which was accompanied by FOOT LOCKER which was also the second highest in sales among the retailers, while WALMART and AMAZON could not make much sales 
Another key finding which i learnt was that the sales are more towards the month of July and August then also on the last month of the years December
And the last finding which I
learnt was that the sales move more online than any other sales method used.
